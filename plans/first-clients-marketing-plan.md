# First Clients Marketing Plan

Goal: sign the first 2–3 paying clients within 90 days.
Constraints: low budget, full-time availability, no existing clients, no case studies. Starting clean, with no material from `growth/`.

## Principle

With zero clients, inbound channels (SEO, LinkedIn audience, free tools) are too slow to be the main engine. Direct founder-led conversations bring the first clients. Inbound channels run in parallel to compound for later.

## Channel split (full-time week)

| Channel | Share of time | Role |
|---------|---------------|------|
| Direct outreach to one vertical (#121, #95) | ~60% | Source of the first clients |
| Referral partners: accountants, system vendors (#56, #137) | ~15% | Warm introductions |
| LinkedIn posts + comment marketing (#39, #44) | ~15% | Credibility and compounding reach |
| Free tool: eSocial validator, assessment quiz (#15, #17) | ~10% | Lead magnet, lead capture |

Deferred until there are cases and data: programmatic SEO (#4), workshops and webinars (#65), paid ads.

## Phase 1 — Foundation (week 1–2)

1. Pick ONE vertical to start. Recommendation: healthcare (clinics), with a first workflow around appointment confirmation and no-show reduction via WhatsApp. Reason: the pain is measurable in reais.
2. Define the pilot offer: free diagnostic (30 min) plus one workflow built at no cost, in exchange for a written testimonial and permission to publish the case. Standard setup is already free, so the pilot costs build time only.
3. Build a target list of 100 clinics in Belo Horizonte (owner or manager name, WhatsApp, Instagram).
4. Prepare one demo workflow that can be shown live in 5 minutes.
5. All client-facing copy goes through the `brand-voice` skill.

## Phase 2 — Outreach and pilots (week 3–8)

1. 10 new conversations per day, mixing WhatsApp, LinkedIn and in-person visits to clinics.
2. Track every lead: contacted, replied, diagnostic held, pilot started, paying.
3. Reach out to 15–20 accountants in BH with a simple partner offer.
4. Post on LinkedIn 3 times per week and comment on 5 relevant posts per day.
5. Deliver the pilots and measure the result (hours saved, no-shows avoided).

## Phase 3 — Convert and publish (week 9–12)

1. Convert pilots into monthly subscriptions.
2. Turn each pilot into a case study with real numbers.
3. Publish the cases on the site and LinkedIn.
4. Review the funnel. Decide whether to double down on the vertical or open a second one.

## Planning assumptions (not benchmarks, adjust with real data)

About 200 contacts → ~20 replies → ~8 diagnostics → ~3 pilots → ~2 paying clients.

## Open decisions

- Confirm the starting vertical (healthcare recommended).
- Confirm the pilot terms (free workflow in exchange for a case study).
